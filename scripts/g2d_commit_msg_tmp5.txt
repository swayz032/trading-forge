worker-1: F36 off-live repair -- async-launch-ack vs true-final-completion boundary

Per AR-1313A. Zero new Agent/Task/model calls; no live guard propagation.

New off-live module src/engine/extraction/g2d_subagentstop_capture.py adds
three functions on top of the existing, UNCHANGED capture_native_return()
doorway:

- record_async_launch_ack(): PostToolUse(Agent) async-ack lands here.
  Launch telemetry only, never creates .raw/.completion, row stays
  NATIVE_TASK_DISPATCHED.
- capture_subagent_stop_final(): the real finalization boundary. Binds by
  agent_id to the recorded launch ack, then calls capture_native_return()
  unchanged -- duplicate-finalization protection is inherited, not
  reimplemented.
- assert_sequential_interlock(): every queue row strictly before `ref`
  must already be RAW_RETURN_CAPTURED before `ref` may be claimed.

(scripts/claude_guard_hook.mjs) and the installed @anthropic-ai/claude-code
package for the real SubagentStop payload schema -- neither documents it.
agent_id/raw_output are therefore taken as opaque caller-supplied
arguments; the state-machine correctness is proven independent of that
open field-name question, which is flagged as the one remaining item
before live wiring.
