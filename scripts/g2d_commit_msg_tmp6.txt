Closes the one open item from the prior F36 off-live packet: "the exact
SubagentStop payload schema is unverified locally." Rather than guess,
fetched Anthropic's own live documentation (docs.claude.com/.../hooks ->
redirects to code.claude.com/docs/en/hooks#subagentstop) and quoted the
schema verbatim.

Confirmed real fields: agent_id, last_assistant_message, hook_event_name,
stop_reason (one of end_turn/max_tokens/stop_sequence/tool_use). The
ruling's original field-name assumption was correct.

One thing the ruling's framing missed that this catches before any live
wiring: stop_reason can be "tool_use" -- the subagent paused awaiting a
tool result, not actually finished. SubagentStop is not guaranteed to fire
only once at true completion. Treating every SubagentStop as terminal
would reintroduce a variant of F36 one layer up. New
extract_subagent_stop_fields() treats only end_turn/max_tokens/
stop_sequence as terminal and raises a distinct SubagentStopNotTerminal
(not a generic failure) on tool_use.
