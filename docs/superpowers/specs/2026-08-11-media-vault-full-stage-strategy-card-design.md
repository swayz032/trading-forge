# Media Vault Full-Stage Strategy Card Hardening

Date: 2026-08-11

## Purpose

Harden the existing Media Vault Compiler View and Paper Fight Night experience after production visual review. The compiler reveal must feel like a Category 5 source-to-strategy transformation, then settle into a calm full-stage strategy card. Fight Night must recover arena height by moving its status metrics into the header.

This work changes presentation only. It does not add a compiler, infer trading logic, change pipeline authority, or fabricate data.

## Compiler View Sequence

Every `Compiler View` activation starts a fresh seven-second sequence and uses the complete main-left stage:

1. `Source lock` expands the selected YouTube evidence plane into the scene.
2. `Extraction rupture` tears real transcript fragments away from the source and accelerates them into depth.
3. `Category 5 vortex` fills the stage with dense particle walls, turbulent fog, lightning arcs, camera pressure, and rapidly increasing rotational velocity.
4. `Core compression` pulls every visual layer into the compiler core.
5. `Shockwave` clears the storm across the full stage.
6. `Strategy reveal` exposes a calm generated environment and the full-stage strategy card.

The storm must read as a violent transformation, not a calm data orbit. Motion remains silent by default. `prefers-reduced-motion`, WebGL loss, and low-power profiles preserve the same truthful sequence and settled information without aggressive motion.

## Generated Environment

The visual system uses a hybrid environment:

- One locally stored, text-free cinematic background plate supplies production-grade scale, material detail, and depth.
- Dependency-free WebGL2 supplies the per-video vortex, lighting, fog, shockwave, market geometry, and settled movement.
- The selected video identifier deterministically controls palette, storm paths, particle rhythm, lightning cadence, camera curve, and settled lighting.
- The local plate cannot contain charts, prices, rules, people, logos, status colors, or performance claims.
- No runtime image-generation call, CDN, remote module, or hidden network dependency is allowed.

Truth colors remain fixed regardless of source identity: lime for verified, amber for derived or framework-supplied, red for refused or unsupported, and steel for unavailable or unbound.

## Full-Stage Strategy Card

After the shockwave, the main stage itself is the strategy card. There is no smaller card inside it, default technical drawer, orbit menu, dead border space, or internal scrollbar.

A narrow top rail contains:

- strategy name;
- symbol, timeframe, and direction;
- persisted compiler state;
- `Technical Receipt`;
- `Media View`.

The main body uses five large trader-facing rule zones:

1. `Trade When`: persisted Context and Setup rules.
2. `Enter`: persisted Entry rules and direction.
3. `Protect`: persisted Stop and Sizing rules.
4. `Manage`: persisted Exit and trade-management rules.
5. `Avoid`: persisted Filters and invalidations.

Each zone displays at most two high-signal persisted rules in plain language and indicates when more persisted rules exist. Known persisted structures may be formatted for readability, but the browser cannot infer a value, invent a missing rule, summarize transcript prose, or alter compiler meaning.

`Technical Receipt` opens the complete persisted evidence in a secondary overlay: provenance, source quotation or span, canonical expression, warnings, and receipt hashes. It is not open by default and does not compete with the strategy card.

## Uncompiled and Failure States

An uncompiled strategy receives the same full Category 5 transformation and premium generated environment. The settled stage becomes a dormant blueprint with:

`SOURCE SECURED - TRADING RULES AWAITING COMPILATION`

It can display only real source identity and transcript metadata. It cannot display an entry, stop, target, size, confidence, progress percentage, or example rule.

Refused, stale, and unavailable states preserve the same full-stage composition with unmistakable truthful status treatments. Stale or unavailable data can never remain visually green.

## Fight Night Header HUD

The standalone aggregate metrics strip below the Fight Night introduction is removed. Its four facts move to one compact telemetry HUD at the upper-right of the Fight Night header, above the header border:

- strategies in arena;
- events seen this screen;
- feed state;
- connection state (`IDLE` or the real active state).

The title and explanatory copy remain on the left. The arena starts immediately below the header and receives the recovered height. Desktop targets remain 1920x1080, 1600x900, 1440x900, and 1366x768 with no page scrolling or ring clipping. Mobile retains normal document scrolling and may stack the HUD below the title.

## Rendering and Performance

- WebGL2 remains dependency-free and lazy-loaded.
- High-end desktop uses an adaptive dense profile; mid-tier and low-power profiles bound particle count and device pixel ratio.
- GPU resources are disposed when Compiler View closes or replays.
- WebGL context loss leaves the DOM strategy card readable.
- Keyboard access reaches `Technical Receipt` and `Media View`.
- Reduced motion crossfades from source evidence into the settled strategy card.

## Verification

- Pure model tests pin deterministic per-video identity and zero fabricated rules.
- Renderer contract tests pin full-stage Category 5 phases, replay, adaptive profiles, fallback behavior, five settled rule groups, and closed-by-default technical receipts.
- UI contract tests pin the right-rail mode switch and full-stage layout.
- Reporting Room tests pin removal of the aggregate strip and placement of the header HUD.
- Browser screenshots verify compiled and uncompiled settled states and Fight Night at all desktop targets.
- TypeScript, relevant Vitest suites, production isolation, 2026 compliance, system-map check, and diff checks run before landing.

## Success Criteria

- The storm occupies the complete stage and feels materially more aggressive than the current orbit sequence.
- The compression and shockwave provide a clear cinematic transition into the settled state.
- The entire settled stage is a readable strategy card with five simple decision groups.
- Technical receipt data remains available without default clutter.
- Every video retains deterministic visual identity.
- Uncompiled strategies receive equal visual quality with zero invented trading rules.
- Fight Night metrics occupy the upper-right header and the arena gains their former vertical space.
