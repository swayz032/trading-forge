revives the corpus by not over-gating. (b) certifies it owes the full stack it was never examined under:
- **Its own Gate-1′-class fidelity adjudication** — multi-rater, independent, on demotion's role changes across ALL 14
  concepts. Demotion has NEVER passed a fidelity adjudication; its 9/9 revival is a mechanism result, not a fidelity
  verdict. The classifier passed 9/9 revival too and was then inverted by exactly this adjudication (the rater-flip). So

  (a demotion that revives by removing a MANDATORY gate is the classifier's failure mode wearing demotion's clothes).
- **Pre-registered behavioral gates on the corrected engine** — the revival set (which pairs revive, at what trade
  counts) is the pre-registered behavioral spec; gates are declared BEFORE the certified run, not read off it.
- **Provenance receipts for every demoted condition** — each role change traceable to its source atom + DRI label +
