## §0 — What the pilot IS (and why it exists)
H1's terminal result: the cheap fully-automatic tier-2 discourse pass cannot be conformantly instrumented at local-model scale (proven by execution, twice). H1 therefore NARROWED to its pre-written economics fork: **fidelity achievable at human-in-loop cost.** The pilot runs the Wave-4-assembled conveyor end-to-end on the SEALED 16-video set at the new economics, produces fidelity certificates, and hands them to H2's frozen two-read rule. The conveyor is now **tier-1 (deterministic detection) + tier-3 (blind adjudication)**; the ~30% margin the retired tier-2 would have handled routes to tier-3.
**The adjudicators are FRESH-CONTEXT AGENTS, not literal humans** — control-gated, blind, per the Wave-1 shakedown protocol (proven 92% inter-rater consistency @ ~1.1K tok/item). 'Human-in-loop cost' = agent-hours per video, NOT dollars/hiring. It kills SCALE ('mine a thousand educators cheaply'), not the MISSION (H2's survivor read fires at ANY N).

## ADDENDUM — F-1 RULING: the two-grade certificate ladder (OPERATOR-AUTHORED 2026-07-12, post-independent-grade, BEFORE the seal breaks)

> Authored by the operator after the Wave-4 independent ablation grade surfaced F-1 (3 of 5 compile-integrity lints check compiled-DSL topology that no stage produces at the certificate layer → they would vacuously PASS in the tier-1+tier-3 pilot conveyor). This is NOT a waiver of §4. It is a two-grade ladder that keeps §4's strictest reading as the standard H2 consumes, while letting the pilot issue an explicitly lesser, honestly-labeled instrument. The pilot §1 QUALITY bar and ECONOMICS measurement above are UNCHANGED; this addendum only fixes what "certificate-grade" means at the pilot vs at H2, and locks the deferred work to the read it protects.

**§A — NO lint ever vacuously passes (non-negotiable repair, ships BEFORE the pilot runs).** Per-lint status becomes 3-state: `PASS` / `FAIL` / `NOT_EVALUATED`. In the pilot conveyor (no compiled topology) the three structural lints (`direction_conflation_lint`, `unsat_sat_check`, `or_alternatives_honored`) AND `causality_lint`'s same-bar-opt-out leg emit **`NOT_EVALUATED`** with a machine-readable reason (`no_compiled_topology`), on EVERY certificate field. A `PASS` on a check that structurally could not fail is a false declaration with paperwork — the four-dormancies disease born into the new instrument on day one. `NOT_EVALUATED` is honest scoping satisfied ON THE ARTIFACT (Law 7), not in a memo beside it. `NOT_EVALUATED` NEVER counts as `PASS` toward any grade.

**§B — PILOT-GRADE = the live subset (the pilot's actual question, not a compromise).** A pilot certificate reaches **pilot-grade** iff every spine condition classifies (tier 1/3) with a verbatim anchor AND the LIVE lints pass: `f2_coverage_gate` + `causality_lint`'s impossible-ref regex leg. The three structural lints + causality's same-bar leg are `NOT_EVALUATED` (§A) and do not gate pilot-grade. The frozen §1 ≥60% bar reads against pilot-grade. This fully serves the pilot's load-bearing questions — extraction fidelity (does every spine condition classify with a verbatim anchor?) and economics (adjudications/video). The certificates come off the line self-describing their scope.

**§C — FULL-GRADE + the binding H2 precondition (A is a hard gate, not a someday).** A **full-grade** certificate = every spine condition classified+anchored AND all FIVE compile-integrity lints `PASS` on REAL compiled topology (nothing `NOT_EVALUATED`). The compile-stage topology producer (option A: wire extraction output through the existing compiler so the assembler reads real `ConditionTopology`/`or_branches`/`same_bar_fill`/`signal_lag`) is a **PRE-REGISTERED PRECONDITION of the H2 battery read** — sequenced BETWEEN pilot and H2, with its own ratify-packet + build/grade loop (mostly wiring through a compiler that already exists). **PINNED NOW, before any pilot number exists:** the H2 pre-reg's "robust survivor … with a fidelity certificate" means a FULL-GRADE certificate. **Every pilot-grade certificate UPGRADES to full-grade before any battery verdict is attributed to its source.** No survivor is EVER read through a scoped certificate. Rationale (why battery-catches-it is insufficient): the battery catching compile-incoherence is not the point — ATTRIBUTION is. A certified-faithful strategy that dies in the battery because the compiler mangled its topology would be recorded as "the educator's strategy failed" when the truth is "our pipeline broke it" — a pipeline artifact masquerading as evidence about the source population, polluting the exact rate read the campaign exists to protect. Compile-fidelity is the second half of the attribution chain the certificate was invented to guarantee.

**§D — Law-4 resolution (strictest reading kept, no waiver).** §4's all-five requirement is NOT relaxed — it is preserved as the standard for FULL-GRADE, the only grade H2 consumes. The pilot issues an explicitly lesser instrument, labeled as such in every field (§A/§B). Strictest reading kept; honest instrument shipped; the escape hatch has a lock (the §C H2 precondition) that the doer did not author and the pilot's numbers cannot pick.

**Direction-check (operator, for the record):** B-repaired keeps the pilot's live path to failure fully intact (the ≥60% bar on the live subset is the real fidelity question, untouched), REMOVES fields that would have flattered certificates (vacuous PASS → NOT_EVALUATED), and converts the deferred work into a HARD GATE on the read it actually protects (the H2 survivor attribution). ## ADDENDUM 2 — SEGMENTATION RULING: the extractor's condition IS the unit (OPERATOR-AUTHORED 2026-07-12, BEFORE the seal breaks)

> Authored after the pilot-conveyor-runner independent grade proved `_segment_transcript` (naive sentence-punctuation split) is an UNREGISTERED INSTRUMENT DECISION that materially moves cert-grade composition + the §1 tier-3-adjudications/video economics number AND silently drops conditions. This is NOT a preference call — it is **F-5's class caught one level up**: mechanical text-slices are a PROXY POPULATION wearing the extractor's clothes. A certificate that asserts fidelity about clause-slices while H2's battery runs on EXTRACTOR OUTPUT would sever the attribution chain the certificate exists to guarantee — the same reasoning that killed the vacuous lints in the F-1 addendum: **the artifact must describe the production thing, not a stand-in.** Option B (a deterministic mechanical clause-rule) is DISQUALIFIED by law. The extractor's condition is the unit; it always was.

**THE UNIT (ruling).** The pilot conveyor is `transcript → production gemma transcript_extractor → extracted strategy + verbatim-anchored spine conditions → tier-1/tier-3 classify each condition's surface + fidelity → compile-lints → certificate`. Tier-1 returns to its GRADED CONTRACT (one condition per call — the 143 design conditions were pre-segmented; it classifies a condition, it does not derive conditions from raw text). The conveyor under test now INCLUDES the thing the campaign exists to test — the LLM entering the conveyor is not a compromise, it is the subject taking its own exam. The frozen §1 bar is unchanged and is what it always described: "a fresh video set reaches certificate-grade extraction."

**Five clauses A needs to be honest (all FROZEN):**

1. **Extractor version-pinned + FROZEN for the pilot.** The production gemma `transcript_extractor` at its current landed SHA is stamped into every certificate's `provenance.extractor_version`. NO extractor changes during the pilot — the pilot measures the CURRENT extractor; improving it is post-read iteration under the extractor's own parity-gated budget (the 5-fixture parity test, CLAUDE.md §2b). Without this clause the pilot silently becomes a tuning loop against the sealed set.

2. **One extraction pass per video, read-once, outputs vaulted immediately.** Non-determinism is a KNOWN, pre-registered property: seed/params pinned; incremental caching so a crash never forces re-extraction ambiguity; if a video's pass must re-run (crash mid-video), the re-run REPLACES that whole video's pass — no cherry-picking between passes (the determinism-envelope precedent). The pilot reads ONE extraction pass per video.

3. **The non-drop invariant lives at BOTH levels (both already built) — no stage where a condition silently disappears.** (a) EXTRACTOR-level coverage = the F-2-fixed `f2_coverage_gate` (transcript-enumerated speaker items vs extraction; the Python lint is parity-fixtured to the exact-token corrected semantic, LIVE in pilot-grade). (b) CLASSIFICATION-level = the runner invariant: every extracted spine condition gets a tier-1 detection OR an explicit `Tier1FallThrough` (→ tier-3) — nothing vanishes. State the pairing explicitly: extraction-covers-the-transcript AND every-extracted-condition-is-classified-or-fell-through.

4. **Unanchored conditions count AGAINST cert-grade, honestly.** The schema demands a verbatim `quote_anchor` + `char_span`; the extraction machinery carries evidence spans, so the runner threads them through. Any condition the extractor emits WITHOUT a locatable verbatim anchor is a FIDELITY FAILURE IN THE MEASUREMENT — not a bug to patch mid-pilot. That is the anchor floor doing its job (claim-scoping baked in, §4).

5. **Failure attribution recorded per-condition (ANNOTATION-TIER; the §1 bar is unchanged).** When a video misses cert-grade, the record says WHERE: coverage-miss / unanchored-condition / classification-fallthrough-unresolved / tier-3-fail / lint-fail. The ≥60% read then arrives WITH its own diagnosis distribution — actionable in either direction — at zero cost, since the assembler already tracks per-condition status. This annotates the verdict; it does not change the aggregate bar.

**Dry-run bonus (noted):** exercising the REAL extractor on a NON-SEALED transcript smoke-tests transcript-fetch AND gemma health in the same rehearsal — two of the three unseal greens (#2 dry-run + #3-transcript-fetch/gemma) checked by one throwaway run.

## ADDENDUM 3 — ANCHOR RULING: the anchor-locator (A-prime — LLM proposes, mechanics verify) (OPERATOR-AUTHORED 2026-07-12, BEFORE the seal breaks)

> Authored after the runner rework proved Addendum-2 clause-4's premise FALSE: the frozen production extractor emits spine conditions WITHOUT verbatim evidence anchors for the fields tier-1 targets (`entry_sequence`/`confluences`/`stop`/`targets` carry no quote; only the vocabulary-only `speaker_concepts` pass carries `transcript_quote`, a category mismatch). The certificate schema §4 requires a verbatim `quote_anchor`+`char_span` per condition → the pilot as-wired would read ~0% cert-grade, a PIPELINE GAP masquerading as fidelity failure. Resolution is **A-prime**, NOT A-as-schema-change and NOT B.
> **Why not A-as-schema-change (adding a per-condition quote field to the extractor):** the schema-IS-the-boundary law (minted 2026-07-12, [[feedback_schema_is_decision_boundary_constrained_sampling_2026_07_12]]) applies to ADDING fields too. Requiring the model to emit a verbatim quote per condition perturbs the constrained-sampling distribution (attention shifts; hard-to-quote conditions drop or reword toward quotable text) — exactly the "good drift in the extractor during the pilot" clause 1's freeze forbids. Amending a pre-registered freeze is precedent-currency not spent when a design exists that needs no amendment.

**A-PRIME — the anchor-locator (a CONVEYOR instrument, not an extractor change):**
1. **Extractor stays FROZEN at its current SHA. Clause 1 UNAMENDED.** It emits exactly what it emits today.
2. **New conveyor component — the anchor-locator:** given (transcript, ONE extracted condition) → return the literal transcript span that grounds it, OR abstain. Local gemma, ~one call per condition, trivial economics. Ships its OWN birth fixtures from birth (locates-known / abstains-on-ungroundable / literal-only) and its OWN independent grade. Same architectural move as the pre-call decline gate — an LLM step whose output a mechanical check owns.
3. **Verification is MECHANICAL + EXACT — this is what makes an LLM locator safe.** The located quote MUST be a literal substring of the transcript (whitespace-normalization ONLY, F-2's exact-token discipline — NOT fuzzy). Hallucinated quote → fails the substring check → UNANCHORED. Locator declines → UNANCHORED. Both failure modes land honestly BY CONSTRUCTION; the gemma no-calibrated-middle finding cannot hurt the measurement because the mechanical check owns the truth.
4. **Anchor-SUPPORTS-condition stays tier-3's job.** The blind adjudicators already read quote-versus-condition as part of fidelity. A mislocated-but-literal anchor is caught by the rater, where judgment belongs — NOT by the locator (which only proves the span is real transcript text).
5. **UNANCHORED counts AGAINST cert-grade, exactly as clause 4 said** — but now it measures something REAL: the conveyor's ability to ground the current extractor's conditions in the trader's words, a legitimate component of the H1 answer rather than a guaranteed-zero artifact.

**A-as-schema-change is RE-SEQUENCED, not rejected:** registered as the POST-PILOT extractor-upgrade candidate ("quoting-as-it-extracts may DISCIPLINE extraction") under its own parity-gated ratify-packet with a before/after extraction-stability check on DESIGN-side data — AFTER the pilot has measured the frozen baseline. Improving the witness is next quarter's work; the pilot measures the witness we have. **B stays DEAD** (moves fuzziness into the verifier, permanently weakening the anchor floor for every future certificate; A-prime keeps the verifier exact forever and puts the burden on a producer whose failures are honest). **C stays DEAD** (~0% read for a pipeline reason = the mismeasurement the seal exists to prevent).

**Sequence:** locator build + birth fixtures → independent grade → the held rework commits with the locator wired as its anchor source (its location code already correct per the rework grade) → rework re-grade → dry-run re-proof on a NON-SEALED transcript (now exercising extractor + locator + fetch + gemma in one rehearsal) → three greens (locator+rework graded · dry-run proven · tower/transcript/gemma verified) + fresh capacity → unseal. Junction cleanup per the pinned rule (remove the LINK, never the tree).
