## 4. NAMED OPTIMISTIC BIAS (R-040 pin 2iii — on every artifact + receipt)
An `approximation=True` condition degrades to pass-through (`np.ones`) in `SpecConditionStrategy` → the condition is effectively UNGATED → the compiled spec trades **LOOSER than taught**. This error direction is **OPTIMISTIC** (the battery could pass a ghost on trades the trader never took). Therefore: every battery verdict on an approximated spec carries its approximation rate in the scope line; a high-approximation spec cannot ground a survivor claim on its own. The producer stamps the rate ON the artifact so the scope line is mechanical, not remembered.
