## THE COMPLETE FROZEN RULE — two independent reads

### SURVIVOR READ — continuous, ANY N (the mission's existence proof)
- **Trigger:** the moment **≥1 ROBUST SURVIVOR** exists — read at any N. If certified extraction #6 passes, the existence proof is met at N=6 and the protocol fires. **Nobody waits for more extractions to "complete the read."** The N_min floor protects the *percentage* from small-denominator noise; it must NEVER delay or dilute the survivor event.
- **ROBUST SURVIVOR (definition FROZEN, no soft center):** passes **EVERY** battery gate (walk-forward, CPCV, PBO, DSR, Monte-Carlo ruin, B14, B15) **at the thresholds standing in the re-baseline's pre-registration** (Phase 0's output), on the corrected engine, **with a fidelity certificate**. NO waivers. NO "passes most." **One pre-named exception path only:** a candidate failing SOLELY on a gate later shown DEFECTIVE (instrument-fault, three-leg-verified) is re-run after the fix — the fix-instrument-then-re-run blade (as Gate 3), NOT a waiver.
- **On a survivor → SURVIVOR-FORENSICS PROTOCOL (pre-registered — success is the pressure this system hasn't faced).** A single battery-passing strategy from a retail-educator corpus is, on priors, more likely an undetected overfit or instrument gap than genuine edge — the base rate null-cal was built to respect. So the scaling protocol's FIRST step is NOT scaling:
  1. **Independent re-verification of the entire chain** — extraction certificate re-adjudicated FRESH; battery re-run from clean state; provenance audit.
  2. **Then** the small-real-capital **graduation test** with pre-registered kill conditions: live performance outside the backtest's confidence bounds within the pre-set window KILLS it (same as any gate).
- **The existence proof is NOT "the battery said yes once." It is: the battery said yes, the forensics held, AND live money didn't falsify it.**

### RATE READ — N ≥ 30 certified extractions ONLY (the conveyor's economics)
- Below N=30: **absolute survivor counts only; rate verdict WITHHELD** (a percentage from a small denominator is noise, not fudged into one).
- At N ≥ 30:
  - **≥5%** of certified extractions are robust survivors → source thesis SUPPORTED → **scale the scout** (educator-mining is productive).
  - **1–5%** → MARGINAL; edge exists but conveyor economics need the differentiation angles (fade-losers, portfolio/correlation).
  - **0 survivors at N≥30** → source thesis FALSIFIED → **scout pivots source-agnostic** (books, papers, event-anchored/microstructure hypotheses); the machinery (engine, certificates, adjudication, taxonomy) transfers INTACT.
- **Asymmetric-stopping trap pre-named:** at N=30 with zero survivors, "just 10 more extractions, the winner might be next" is hope wearing a stopping rule's clothes. **N=30, zero survivors, pivot.** The read fires.

### CALIBRATION HONESTY (in the rule's own text)
5%/1% are **priors-labeled CALIBRATION ESTIMATES, not derived laws.** Retail-educator edges survive rigorous validation at plausibly LOW rates; even 1–5% may be a genuine find, and **~0% is an EXPECTED-POSSIBLE outcome, not a shock.** A grim number CONFIRMS a stated prior. **NO retroactive re-reading in EITHER direction — including the favorable one.**

The campaign now cannot lie at any phase, in either direction — including the direction where it works.
