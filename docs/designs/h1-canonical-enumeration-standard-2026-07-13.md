# H1 — CANONICAL ENUMERATION STANDARD (operator-ruled 2026-07-13)

> The single definition of "how many strategies does a video teach", reconciled to already-frozen campaign law. Neither ontology (the old fixtures' runnable-trigger rubric, nor the instrument's config-groups rule) is adopted wholesale. These THREE RULES are canonical. They resolve to the strictest reading on BOTH diseases — no bar softens.

## RULE 1 — EVIDENCE FLOOR (anti-fabrication, §13's physics)
A strategy object exists ONLY where the transcript teaches **runnable entry logic** (a concrete, executable entry). A hand-wavy mention never gets promoted to an object — it is recorded as an **unpromoted mention**. Elaboration-beyond-evidence at the strategy level is the named fabrication disease.

## RULE 2 — OPPOSITION ALWAYS SPLITS (anti-merge-silencing, the R5L890/fence law)
Teachings with **contradictory executable logic** — opposite directions in the same context, mutually-exclusive entries — MUST be separate objects, **no exceptions**, even sharing levels/indicator/timeframe/one exit. A fused contradictory object is structurally broken even when every condition is preserved (certified CRIT this week).

## RULE 3 — COMPATIBLE VARIATION IS FLEXIBLE, CONTENT IS NOT (§10a/§11-guard-2, verbatim "content is the bar; ontology stays moot")
Timeframe / confirmation-mechanic / target variants may group as variants-within-one OR split — **either is faithful — IFF every variant's distinctives survive as separately-attributed, compilable content.** The gate hunts silenced content, never integer aesthetics.

## THE TEST
*"Could a trader run both teachings at once without contradiction?"* — YES → compatible variation (Rule 3, integer free, content must survive); NO → opposition (Rule 2, must split); a teaching with no runnable entry → not an object (Rule 1, unpromoted mention).

## ADJUDICATION OUTPUT (windows, not bare integers)
Re-mint targets are WINDOWS with content conditions, e.g. "1-or-2 with variant-B distinctives surviving as attributed content", "exactly 2 (opposition forced)", "exactly 1 (the breakdown is a Rule-1 mention)". A blind adjudication returns: the window, WHY (which rule decides), and the content conditions that any faithful extraction must carry.

*This standard reconciles to frozen law: §10a/§11-guard-2 (ontology moot where content survives), §13 (fabrication taxed), the merge-silencing fence (opposition-fusion = CRIT). The integer-match birth table was the drift; these three rules are the restoration.*
