## Plain-English for the operator
Today, on a healthy account, when your 2% risk math says "only trade 1 contract here" (tight stop / small buffer), the bot OVERRIDES that and trades your base size (up to 9) anyway — it treats base size as a floor that beats the risk limit. You chose (D9) to make the risk limit always win: if the math says 1, trade 1; if it says less than 1, skip the trade. This is more honest about risk, but it's the reverse of an earlier choice you made (bumping the drawdown-room % from 1% to 8%) that existed to stop the bot being strangled to 0 contracts. So on a fresh combine with a wide stop, the bot will sometimes trade smaller or skip where it used to trade base. The A/B report that ships with this shows exactly how often that happens, so you can see the real effect and reverse it in one command if it's too strict.
