## Plain-English for the operator
Right now, when the bot manages a trade on paper, it uses a stop that's 33% wider (2× ATR) than the stop your backtest tested and your position-sizer budgeted for (1.5× ATR). So a strategy is graded, sized, and then traded on three slightly different risk geometries — the paper P&L that would promote it isn't measured on the same trade the backtest approved. This fixes all three to ONE honest geometry: the managed stop and the backtest match exactly, and the position size is always budgeted for the actual stop the trade carries (never risking more than your 2% per trade). Nothing is live, so this only re-bases forward paper/backtest evidence — you'll want to re-run flagged backtests before comparing their dollar numbers. A machine-checked parity test between the TypeScript and Python sides ships with it so the two can never silently drift again.
