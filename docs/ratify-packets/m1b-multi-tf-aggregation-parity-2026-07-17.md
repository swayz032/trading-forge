## 0. How this was found (traceability — verify before trusting)

A bar-flow trace (2026-07-17, read-only Explore investigation) was dispatched to answer: does live PAPER-state signal evaluation aggregate raw Massive 1-minute bars into a strategy's configured timeframe (5m/15m/etc.) before evaluating indicators, matching what the backtester does? Findings, independently spot-checked against the live tree by the parent session:

- **Live side — no aggregation exists.** `src/server/routes/paper.ts:201` calls `startStream(session.id, symbols)` — no timeframe argument at all. `paper-trading-stream.ts`'s `ensureSocket()`/`startStream()` create a raw per-symbol WS subscription to Massive's 1-minute `AM` aggregates only (confirmed: M1a's own adapter doc explicitly states "no second-aggregate (A.) bar-builder" by design). `barBuffer` (`paper-trading-stream.ts:56,122,169-192`) is a rolling window of up to 200 **raw 1-minute** bars, passed straight to `evaluateSignals()` (`paper-trading-stream.ts:380`) → `computeIndicators(barBuffer)` (`paper-signal-service.ts:1251,2296,6516`) — no timeframe parameter anywhere in this call chain. The ONLY place `session.timeframe` is consulted in the live signal path is `getBarDurationMs()` (`paper-signal-service.ts:1929-1943`), consumed solely by `setCooldown()` (`:1945-1948`) for a post-trade cooldown window — not for resampling bars or gating indicator computation.
- **Backtest side — genuinely timeframe-native.** `src/engine/backtester.py:3652-3654` (and the DSL entry point, `:6656,6681-6682`) call `load_ohlcv(config.symbol, config.timeframe, ...)`, which (`src/engine/data_loader.py:868-883`) reads from a pre-partitioned, timeframe-native Parquet source (`s3://{bucket}/futures/{symbol}/consolidated/{timeframe}.parquet`) — one file per symbol/timeframe, not resampled at request time from 1-minute data.
- **Live incidence check (DB query run against production DB, 2026-07-17):** of 120 total strategies, 117 are CANDIDATE and 3 NEEDS_ARCHETYPE — **zero strategies are currently in TESTING/SHADOW/PAPER or beyond.** So the bug is producing zero corrupted evidence *today* — nothing has been promoted far enough to be affected yet. However, of the 120 strategies' configured timeframes: `5m: 78, 15m: 15, 4h: 9, 1m: 9, 1h: 6, 30m: 3` — **111 of 120 (92.5%) are non-1m.** The instant any of these strategies is promoted into PAPER and the Massive paid feed begins qualifying days, this bug fires for the overwhelming majority of the pipeline.

## 1. What & why

A strategy configured with `timeframe: "5m"` (or 15m/30m/1h/4h) is, in live paper trading, evaluated on **every raw 1-minute bar** — its indicators (EMA/ATR/etc. lookback windows, cooldown-in-bars, `max_hold_bars`, any bar-counted state) are computed over 1-minute increments regardless of what the strategy's config declares. The backtest that validated this strategy's edge, by contrast, loaded genuinely-aggregated N-minute OHLC bars. This is not a cosmetic mismatch — it is a **unit-of-time divergence**: an `EMA(21)` intended as 21×5-minute bars (105 minutes of lookback) becomes, live, 21×1-minute bars (21 minutes of lookback) — a >4x distortion — plus completely different intrabar signal timing (a live "5m" strategy fires 5x more often than intended, on every 1-minute close instead of every 5-minute close) and incompatible bar-counted cooldown/hold-time semantics.

**Why this matters for the campaign specifically:** the entire point of this campaign is to make PAPER's evidence TRUE before the operator pays for the Massive feed and starts a 5-10-day qualifying window (D5/D6/D7). If this bug ships unfixed, ~92.5% of the strategy pool would generate PAPER evidence that does not reflect what was backtested — a strategy could "qualify" on a completely different (and untested) 1-minute-reactive variant of itself, or a good strategy could be spuriously killed by noise its real (aggregated) timeframe would have filtered out. This is exactly the class of silent producer→consumer divergence the campaign's Wiring-Verification Protocol exists to catch.

## 2. Severity & urgency (operator decision point)

- **Not urgent by live-capital or in-flight-evidence measure** — zero strategies are currently past CANDIDATE, so no PAPER evidence exists yet to be corrupted, and nothing is live. This is NOT a hold-everything emergency.
- **Urgent by pipeline-blocking measure** — with 111/120 strategies non-1m, this must land BEFORE the first strategy is promoted into PAPER on the paid feed, or the qualifying-day evidence for the vast majority of the pipeline is meaningless from day one. Recommend this gates the **PAYMENT GATE** conceptually (not technically — M1a + W1 already satisfy the plan's literal payment-gate text) in the same spirit: paying for Massive and beginning a qualifying week while this bug is live would waste the shakedown week's evidence for any non-1m strategy that reaches PAPER during it.
- Recommend sequencing: implement before any strategy is promoted past SHADOW into PAPER, ideally before or during the first paid shakedown week — not deferred to W7 close-out.

## 3. Proposed fix design (for the future M1c implementation wave — NOT built by this packet)

Add a client-side bar aggregator between the raw Massive 1-minute stream and `evaluateSignals()`:
- New pure module, e.g. `src/server/lib/timeframe-bar-aggregator.ts`: given a stream of 1-minute bars and a target timeframe string (`"5m"`/`"15m"`/`"30m"`/`"1h"`/`"4h"`), buckets incoming 1-minute bars into the target timeframe's boundary-aligned windows (aligned to session/RTH open per the same convention `toGlobexSessionDateString`/session-boundary helpers already use — verify against how the backtest's own bar-bucketing treats partial/incomplete final bars), and emits a completed N-minute OHLC bar (open = first bucket's open, high/low = bucket max/min, close = last bucket's close, volume = sum) only once the bucket's window has fully closed — never emits a partial/in-progress bar to the strategy evaluator.
- Wire into `paper-trading-stream.ts`: `ensureSocket()`/`handleBar()` gains an aggregation step per-session (keyed by `session.timeframe`, so two sessions on the same symbol but different timeframes each get correctly independent aggregation) between raw-bar receipt and `evaluateSignals()` invocation. 1m-timeframe strategies pass through unaggregated (byte-identical to today — regression-safety anchor).
- `computeIndicators`/`getBarDurationMs`/cooldown logic: audit for any remaining raw-1m-bar assumption once bars are properly pre-aggregated upstream (should mostly "just work" once fed correctly-bucketed bars, but must be verified, not assumed).
- Parity test: same historical 1-minute tape, run through (a) the new live aggregator + `evaluateSignals`, and (b) the backtester's `load_ohlcv` timeframe-native bars for the same symbol/timeframe/date range → indicator values and signal-firing bars must match (or a documented, bounded tolerance for boundary-timing effects — RED-proof: revert the aggregator, same test must fail).
- Explicitly OUT of scope for M1c (do not build): a server-side/Massive-API-level second-aggregate reconciler (the campaign's existing "Explicitly OUT of scope" section already rejects this — "candle strategies need minute bars; complexity serves fill realism Starter can't certify" — this fix is a LOCAL client-side resampler of already-received 1-minute bars, not a request for Massive to pre-bucket on its end, so it does not conflict with that prior decision).

## 4. Blast radius (for the future M1c wave)

- Any strategy promoted to TESTING/SHADOW/PAPER+ with `timeframe != "1m"` going forward gets DIFFERENT (corrected) live signal-firing behavior — this is the entire point, but it means **any strategy already sitting in TESTING/SHADOW at the time M1c lands should have its evidence window treated as reset/restarted** (same "operator restarts in-flight evidence windows" disposition as W1/W2's landing note) — confirmed currently moot (zero strategies past CANDIDATE today), but must be checked again at M1c's actual landing time since state may have changed.
- `test:metrics` golden fixtures: backtest-side untouched (no aggregation change on that side) — expect UNCHANGED; any shift is the usual reserved-class HOLD trigger.
- 1-minute-timeframe strategies (9 of 120 today): byte-identical behavior before/after — a required regression anchor, not just a convenience.

## 5. Verification plan (for the future M1c wave)

(a) Unit tests on the pure aggregator: boundary alignment, partial-bucket-never-emitted, volume-sum correctness, multi-session-same-symbol-different-timeframe independence. (b) Cross-language parity test vs backtester's `load_ohlcv` bars (RED-proof: revert aggregator → parity test fails). (c) 1m-passthrough regression test (byte-identical to pre-fix). (d) tsc + touched vitest + relevant pytest + 3 CI gates + system-map:sync. (e) Independent doer≠grader accuracy-validator grade — specifically checking the boundary-alignment edge cases (session open, DST transitions, weekend/holiday gaps interacting with bucket boundaries) since this is exactly the kind of off-by-one class that produces silent, hard-to-notice drift.

## 6. Rollback (for the future M1c wave)

Single-commit revert; no migration; no data rewrite. Any strategy that had reached PAPER on the corrected aggregation would need its evidence window flagged/reset on rollback, same as landing.

## 7. Independent grade (accuracy-validator, doer≠grader, 2026-07-17)

**CONFIRMED, VERIFIED BAND 8** — every factual claim in this packet re-verified independently against live code and a fresh DB query, matching the original trace's citations and numbers exactly (DB query reproduced 120/117/3/lifecycle and 78/15/9/9/6/3/timeframe digit-for-digit). No aggregation step was found anywhere in the live path on a broadened repo-wide search; the backtest's timeframe-native Parquet mechanism was independently confirmed.

**Additional finding from the grader, not in the original trace:** a candidate mitigating safeguard was checked and ruled out — the Wave 29 Pass A.3 shadow-signal-divergence gate (`shadow-signal-divergence-checker.ts`, wired into both the cron and manual promotion paths) does **not** prevent this bug's harm. SHADOW-mode evaluation runs through the identical buggy `computeIndicators(barBuffer)` call (the shadow intercept sits downstream of it, `paper-signal-service.ts:5984-6173`), a direct legacy `TESTING→PAPER` edge bypasses SHADOW entirely (`lifecycle-service.ts:323`), and the gate has never executed against real data (0 completed backtests, 0 `lifecycle_shadow_signals` rows in production today). In every branch — gate fires, gate bypassed, or gate never evaluates — the paid week's evidence for non-1m strategies is compromised in some form; at best the failure mode shifts from "silent corruption" to "opaque stall behind an unrelated-looking divergence error," which is arguably harder to diagnose mid-shakedown-week, not easier. This does not change the packet's recommendation — it strengthens the case for prioritizing M1c before the first paid week rather than relying on downstream gates to catch it.

## Plain-English for the operator

**A real bug, found today, currently harmless, but big once qualification starts.** Strategies configured for 5-minute or 15-minute charts are, in live paper trading, actually being evaluated on every 1-minute tick instead — a completely different (and never-backtested) trading style. Right now this affects nothing, because zero strategies have been promoted far enough to be exposed to it. But 111 of your 120 strategies are configured for something other than 1-minute charts, so the moment you start paying for the live feed and strategies start qualifying, this bug would silently make most of the qualifying evidence meaningless. **Recommendation: build the fix (a small local component that properly buckets 1-minute bars into the strategy's real timeframe before evaluating it) before or during the first paid shakedown week — not after.** This packet documents the fix design; a follow-up wave builds and tests it.
